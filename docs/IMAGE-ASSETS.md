# Image asset notes

`background.png`

- Static image; 1920×1080 (16:9) recommended.
- The theme scales it to the current display size.
- Avoid text or important details at the extreme edges, since aspect ratios
  differ between displays.

`micalet-logo.png`

- PNG with transparency recommended.
- The image is centered and constrained to 30% of the screen width and height.
- Its current opacity range in the script is 0.88 to 1.0. This modulation is subtle and may not be perceptible in every environment. There are no frame images
  and no movement of the logo.
